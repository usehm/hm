# Hm trademark policy

Hm's source code is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The license covers the code; it does not grant any rights to the name **"Hm"**, the **Hm logo** or the **usehm.dev** domain (the "Marks"), which belong to Mohsen Mirzakhani.

This policy keeps the Marks meaning one thing: the software published by the Hm project. It is meant to be easy to follow. When in doubt, ask.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name "Hm" to refer to the project truthfully: in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews and comparisons.
- Redistribute **unmodified** official builds or source under the name Hm, for example in a Linux distribution's package repository.
- Say that your software works with Hm, is a plugin, theme or extension for Hm, or is "based on Hm", as long as it is clear it is not made or endorsed by the Hm project.
- Use the logo to link to usehm.dev or the Hm repository.

## You may not, without written permission

- Distribute a **modified** version of Hm under the name "Hm" or with the Hm logo. Forks are welcome under the AGPL, but must use a different name and logo and remove the Marks from the app, its icon and its "About" information.
- Use the Marks, or a confusingly similar name or logo, in the name of your product, company, domain or social media account.
- Use the Marks in a way that suggests the Hm project sponsors, endorses or is affiliated with you.
- Change the logo, or combine it with other marks.

## Asking for permission

Open an issue at [github.com/usehm/hm](https://github.com/usehm/hm/issues) or contact the maintainer through GitHub.
